# PDF regression

Original-code fictional PDFs were recaptured directly from the legacy app at `http://localhost:4001`. Served source, fonts, logo and pdfmake bytes were verified against original GitHub commit `aa8599a19ca8045ff924428609084a4070e6ae6d` before capture. The generator/template fixtures are unmodified original files. [Provenance](legacy-sample-provenance.json) records source hashes, inputs, print clock and generated PDF hashes, with no source transformations. Eight baseline samples cover all four categories in Thai/English; two compact original referrals cover all eleven modules for wording review. The six user-supplied original PDFs are preserved unchanged and recorded separately, with unknown input details clearly distinguished. All references are in [samples/legacy](samples/legacy).

The user subsequently approved a shared Sarabun family, 9 pt clinic-name lines and 6.5 pt institutional/footer text. Consequently, the previous requirement for byte-identical typography has been superseded for those changes, the subsequently requested clinic-name spelling and the replacement clinic logo only. Regression compares the new pages with a reference made from the original generator with only those authorised font changes, the subsequently requested English header name “Public Health Center 28 Krung Thon Buri” (removing the legacy hyphen), and the supplied replacement logo. Its height stays at 223 × 1182 / 5300 = 49.73245 pt; width follows the supplied 800 × 202 aspect ratio. PNG embedding is local and lossless. The original image and historical samples remain unchanged. Approved replacement-logo pixels, other header/footer wording, margins, contact information, line rules, printed-date format and page-number format are independently retained. Historical fixtures and legacy sample PDFs remain unchanged.

The six user-supplied PDF references remain local and are excluded from Git because their inputs are not documented as fictional. Their filenames and hashes remain in the provenance report; generated fictional samples and original source fixtures are versioned for reproducible regression checks.

`npm run test:pdf` generates twelve current samples with a fixed fictional print date: all four categories in Thai/English plus treatment-only medical certificates and compact referral wording samples in both languages. It checks A4 dimensions, validation, searchable Thai/English text, no generated N/A/editor hints, non-destructive generation, and each page’s header/static footer at 144 dpi against the approved reference. Every medical certificate must keep the title Medical Certificate / ใบรับรองแพทย์; treatment-only samples exclude examination, fitness-for-work and scheduled-injection assertions. Referrals are checked for inline purposes, the latest laboratory results enclosure, full scheduled Benzathine penicillin G wording, and absence of redundant note content/labels. Unit/browser checks verify the diagnosis/fact/schedule bullet depths and the restricted summary catalogue. Default bullet paragraphs and lists use 2 pt after-spacing, preserving explicit formatting and signing space. The complete referral wording samples occupy two pages each. All templates include an empty paragraph after the title; unit/browser checks verify it remains in PDF conversion. English referral titles use Title Case and all current samples verify the exact clinic spelling. Laboratory enclosure text is an editable structured field; edited/omitted content exports without field highlighting. Doxycycline’s separate duration paragraph is absent. Only the changing page/count numerals are masked in the footer comparison. The replacement logo is included in the whole-header pixel comparison against the approved reference.

Every current header is compared against the approved reference, including replacement-logo pixels. `pdfimages -list` independently verifies the displayed image height against the original legacy PDFs within 0.2 pt (the tool rounds pixel density to whole numbers). Browser tests measure the original display height and new aspect ratio directly.

Machine results: [pdf-regression-results.json](pdf-regression-results.json). Current samples: [samples/current](samples/current). Raster intermediates and the approved reference are generated under ignored `pdf-results`.

Thai body text now uses native word segmentation and empty inline runs to give pdfmake natural wrapping opportunities. No spaces, invisible characters or forced line breaks are added to the wording; field values, formatting and editor state remain unchanged. The official header/footer bypass this body conversion. Thai referral regressions specifically verify that the financial request continues on its opening line after “ทั้งนี้ หากเห็นสมควร”, and that the exported adherence label is “ART adherence”. The implementation uses [ECMA-402 word segmentation](https://tc39.es/ecma402/2024/#sec-segmenter-objects) with pdfmake's [inline text-break handling](https://github.com/bpampuch/pdfmake/blob/0.3.9/src/TextBreaker.js).

Every sample also verifies the requested closing and plain physician name on the same final page, at least 40 pt between their text bounds for signing, and absence of signature/name labels and dotted lines. These measured checks complement the 44 pt blank paragraph defined by the templates.

All four default signatures now include one empty paragraph before ขอแสดงความนับถือ / Yours faithfully, as well as the original 44 pt signing space after it. The leading paragraph is part of the protected-from-splitting body signature group, with 8 pt after-spacing; it does not change the official footer. Unit/browser checks verify its presence in Thai/English and PDF conversion. Insertion buttons participate in keyboard navigation but remain excluded from PDF content.

The regular PDF body is text; only deliberately inserted pictures and the approved header logo are images. Editor highlighting and dropdown controls are not exported. Pictures use local decoded pixels, retain aspect ratio and occupy one unbreakable PDF block with an optional caption. Image insertion/export and removal/Undo are also tested in the browser.

Editor and PDF page counts may differ because empty optional phrases are visible while editing and disappear on export, and browser/pdfmake line metrics differ. No claim of pixel-perfect body-layout parity is made. PDF preview is the final layout check. Native operating-system print dialogues and physical printers were not automated.
