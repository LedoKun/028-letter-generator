# Section migration

All eleven original disease/history modules remain medically distinct. Referral starts with none of them. Referral purposes are inline in its opening paragraph; custom purpose is an inline Other choice, with conditional validation. The medical-certificate title defaults to Medical Certificate / ใบรับรองแพทย์. All templates offer translated title presets and custom titles; actual-treatment sections remain distinct from examination and leave. ART, OI prophylaxis and medication continuity fields can be used within HIV or independently. HIV initially offers a nested ART list, optional prophylaxis and an always-included medication continuity bullet with editable today/60-day defaults. The eleven original diagnoses/histories and the new current-TB, positive serum CrAg and suspected CMVR blocks share the summary group and its restricted insertion point. Current TB remains distinct from completed TB treatment. Diagnoses are outer bullets; their facts, medicines and injection schedule have further indentation. Independent medicines, requests and enclosures remain separate. Latest laboratory results is included by default as the editable attachments.lab field; default text follows the letter language and manual text is retained. Generic referral patient/administrative and Other Medications canvas buttons are removed, while the toolbar and in-memory group destinations preserve those workflows. Closing-request choices are admission/financial only. Travel and free-form closing points are removed; free-form toolbar insertion still targets its editable body. Redundant notes blocks and child note placeholders were removed by explicit user request; old canonical note values remain compatible. Every section supports removal, restoration, editing and accessible Move Up/Move Down controls within its group; archives and canonical values are independent of visibility. Reinserted sections retain their manual wording and field formatting, including after template changes. Native history uses record-specific patches to protect unrelated shared edits. “Start selected sections afresh” explicitly clears the selected section’s values and restores its default prose.

The unified Insert menu includes Writing actions and all clinical modules plus whole identification/date/title/addressee/purpose/physician/CD4/viral-load groups. Clinical insert choices show descriptive English names, accept Thai/English names in search, and remain browsable without keywords. Document title presets follow the letter language. The slash shortcut opens Insert only in an empty paragraph or on a keyboard-focused insertion point; literal and pasted slashes remain text. Insertion controls and auxiliary prose slots take no document space. Referral and free-form letters start with three editable blank writing lines before the contact sentence, preserved in the PDF. Clinical information lists diagnoses/findings; HIV details, treatment/follow-up and patient/document blocks have separate groups. Title choices use the same searchable radio catalogue as Sex, with an Other entry for custom titles. Clipboard slices omit hidden values, session metadata, UI prompts and internal zero-width placeholders while preserving selected text and hard breaks. Advanced field actions and section tools are collapsed in the sidebar. Clickable insertion points and field × buttons never print. Removed occurrences retain canonical values and support Undo/explicit restoration. Ordinary identification follows the letter headers. Required travel medication rows remain subject to final-export validation even if the list is removed. Local PNG/JPEG pictures support proportional pointer/keyboard resizing, captions and removal/Undo, with type/dimension/size validation and local metadata removal. Medical leave uses inclusive calendar days; administered syphilis treatment never imports scheduled Benzathine penicillin G dates.
